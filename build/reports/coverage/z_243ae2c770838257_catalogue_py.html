1"""The canonical catalogue. 

2 

3vehicle_variant is the centre of the system. Everything resolves to it, every 

4verdict is keyed by it, and its specifications provide both the hard 

5constraints used during matching and the ground truth used to check claims. 

6 

7The reason variant granularity matters, and model granularity does not, is on 

8the model page of the prototype: the Creta diesel manual and the Creta turbo 

9automatic sit 1.1 points apart. Averaging them produces the number least 

10useful to someone choosing between them. 

11"""
