Preferences

There are no accounts on Revix and there is nothing to sign up for. These settings live in your own browser, so they follow this device and nothing about you is stored on our side.

Default weighting

Which strategy every verdict page opens with. You can still flip it on any page.

Show the range

The confidence range around every score. Turning it off makes the product look more certain than it is, so it is on by default and we would rather you left it on.

Order topics by

Disagreement first is the default, because the contested topic is usually the one that decides your purchase.

Units

Fuel efficiency and distance.

Price shown as

On-road price varies by state, so ex-showroom is the honest default.

Appearance

Follows your system setting unless you choose otherwise.

Not built, and deliberately so. Accounts, saved shortlists, price alerts and personalised source trust are all out of scope for this project. The weighting system could support personalised trust one day, since a preference is just another weighting configuration, but shipping the measured version of the core product matters more than adding features to it.
