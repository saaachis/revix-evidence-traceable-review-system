The central experiment

Does trust weighting beat star averaging?

We take verified owners with over twelve months and over 10,000 km, average their opinion, and call that the target. Those reviews are then removed entirely. From what is left, which is ordinary mixed-quality review text, we draw a sample and try to predict the target. Lower error is better. This is not circular, because the target is defined by information the estimate never sees.

The honest caveat, stated before anyone asks. The fourth row removes the ownership duration and verified-owner features. Performance drops but stays ahead of equal weighting, which means the text and behaviour signals carry real weight on their own. Without that row the result would be partly circular, and a reviewer who spots that before we do costs us more than the caveat does.
